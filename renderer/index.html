Connect Joggl to Jira

Joggl logs your time straight onto Jira Cloud issues. It needs your Jira site, the email address on your Atlassian account, and an API token. Nothing is sent anywhere else, and the token is encrypted by Windows before it is stored.

The address you use to open Jira in your browser.
Create one at id.atlassian.com/manage-profile/security/api-tokens — click Create API token, give it any label, then copy the value. It is shown only once.
